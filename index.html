Official Rules Last Updated: May 31, 2026
Equipment

A Spikeball™ net and ball are used for official tournament play. The net tension should be consistent.

Court Setup

A circular net is placed at the center. Serving typically occurs from behind a service boundary (commonly ~6 feet from the net).

Serving

The serve is hit from behind the service line into the net. Serves must be below the receiver’s raised hand; the receiver may call “let” on certain issues.

Rally Scoring

Spikeball uses rally scoring. Games are commonly played to 21, win by two (tournament formats can vary).

Hits

Each team has up to three hits per possession. Hits must alternate between teammates; the ball must be hit (not caught/lifted/thrown).

Faults

Common faults include invalid serves and failing to return the ball legally within three hits.

Possession Rules

Possession changes when the ball contacts the net in specific ways (and other rule infractions result in a point loss and change of possession).

Net Contact Rules

After the serve, the ball must bounce off the net in a single bounce, clearing the rim to be good.

Interference Rules

Interference (e.g., hindering an opponent) can result in a penalty. If players cannot determine if the ball hit the rim, replay the point.

Tournament Rules

Tournament directors may specify formats (scoring to a certain point, tie rules, and service/let procedures). Follow the official event rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

For “let” and rim/pocket situations, consult the official rules linked below and agree on interpretations before play.
